01 Genetics Support Tool

Clearer guidance at the moment of decision.

A recurring problem. A revised question set. Practical support for the people doing the work.

Investigating recurring errors

Experienced agents were making recurring errors while processing genetics prior-authorization requests, despite training and extensive job aids. My investigation pointed to guidance that was difficult to apply under processing-time constraints.

I classified error records and reviewed individual root-cause narratives to identify decisions that needed clearer support. From those findings, I drafted questions to guide agents through the process.

Collaboration changed the solution

I took my initial questions to the Nurse Supervisor whose team reported the errors. Together, we refined and expanded the question set to better target the problem.

I authored the operational content: questions, answers, corrective callouts, email templates, and decision logic expressed in pseudocode. AI converted that pseudocode into the local HTML/CSS implementation.

From implementation to everyday use

I rolled out the tool to all teams at the beginning of July, demonstrated it to the genetics teams, and provided team leads with separate guidance and written instructions.

After rollout, team leads confirmed continued use. Leaders described using the tool to coach agents, and agents independently shared positive feedback.

03 Synthetic Genetics Governance Report

Checking the meaning behind the metrics.

A report can reconcile correctly and still lead to the wrong conclusion if its populations or definitions are unclear.

This four-page Power BI project makes population definitions, data-quality checks, metric conflicts, and publication conditions visible using synthetic data.

I directed the project and personally reviewed the synthetic data, error definitions, and population metrics. AI-assisted work supported analysis and implementation.

The report shows how different definitions can change a result—and why a passed check does not automatically make a metric trustworthy.
